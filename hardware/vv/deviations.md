# Deviations from NASA's figures

Why: the register's policy (README, 'Sources and deviations'; Alex, 2026-10-30) is to use the NASA standard but use some discretion where adhering to it would be adversely cost or effort intensive compared to another standard. NASA allows that itself: EEE-INST-002 section 6.5 (eee-inst-002.txt:521) says "Developer-controlled derating plans may be used upon acceptable review and project approval", and Level 3 parts are "intended for mission applications where the use of high-risk parts is acceptable" (eee-inst-002.txt:351). This board's parts are treated as Level 3.

One row per place the register's number departs from NASA's. Every `standard:` or `datasheet:` Numbers entry that departs from NASA says `departs from NASA` and names its DEV-nn row (tools/check_trace.py checks the link, in warning mode). Status PROPOSED: Alex signs a deviation like a requirement, with a line in releases/APPROVALS.md (Claude writes none). Line cites are the `pdftotext -layout` lines of hardware/ref_design/datasheets/eee-inst-002.pdf (NASA/TP-2003-212242, April 2008, Addendum 1 incorporated; NASA's own copy, SOURCES.md).
