| REQ-TST-05 | The PVT lot's first-pass yield on the self-test shall be 90% or more, with every failure logged as a defect. | If many units fail the first self-test, the problem is the build process, not single bad units, and fixing units one at a time hides it. 90% of 30 is 27 units. The 90% is Claude's estimate, with no past yield data on record. |
| REQ-TST-06 | Each unit's cell-reading gain shall be set in production test at a true 3.50 V from the bench supply (no USB, the self-test at its highest load, a DMM checked that session against a 4.096 V reference) and lie within 0.95-1.05; a re-read at 3.50 V shall be within 10 mV; the firmware shall store no gain from a no-cell end-of-charge edge. | The charger's end voltage is only +-1% (+-42 mV), and with no cell its charge-done signal toggles on its own, so a gain taken from that edge could be off by the whole cutoff margin and shut the unit off early or late into the cell's protection trip (DF-225). A re-read within 10 mV is about a quarter of 42 mV; the 10 mV and 0.95 to 1.05 are Claude's. |
| REQ-TST-07 | No shipped unit shall have any connector (the cell's, the panel's, the speaker's) mated more than 50% of its rated cycles, counted per unit. | Cell, panel and speaker connectors are rated for few matings. The panel's FH12 is rated 20 (datasheet), so 10 leaves half its life after bring-up. A worn connector gives intermittent contact, a fault that returns. The other plugs' ratings are not on file; the 50% is Claude's choice. |
| REQ-TST-08 | Every finding on a built unit shall be recorded with its serial, how it was found, its disposition (rework, scrap or use as is) and its re-test; a finding on 2 units shall become a DEF with a check. | A rework with no re-test, or a process fault that recurs on several units and is never raised, ships bad units. defects.md covers design defects only. Two units is the smallest count that shows a pattern, Claude's pick; there is no other reason on record. |
