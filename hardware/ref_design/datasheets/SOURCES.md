The SPICE models for board/sim (Microchip's MCP6001/2/4 macromodel, Alpha & Omega's AO3400)
are not committed either: `board/sim/models.py` fetches and translates them.

`RPI-RP2350B-MINIMAL_LICENSE.txt` is committed: the RP2350B core's footprints and 3D models in
board/lib (the QFN80, crystal and inductor) come from Raspberry Pi's minimal design under that licence, so it
stays as long as they do.

microSD slot: `hirose_dm3_catalog_C114218.pdf` is Hirose's DM3 catalog (May 2017) as LCSC C114218 carries it, DM3AT-SF-PEJM5 on page 3
(https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/ed7c38214cbd45158a701879217600e9.pdf?productCode=C114218; hirose.com refused the
download).

`rocpu_tps850.pdf`: ROCPU TPS 850 series, top actuated (2 pages; LCSC C2759975, the J14K 250 gf 1.4 mm), copied from the power-button analysis's download
(scratchpad esp32_pwrbtn/ds/C2759975): land drawing on page 2, which battler:Tact_ROCPU_TPS850 (tools/fp_tact.py) follows.

Not obtained, 2026-10-30: the UN Manual of Tests and Criteria (Rev. 8, section 38.3): unece.org answers an automated download with HTTP 403. The register's UN38.3 entries stay as they were. The IEC 62368-1 edition 1 (2010) text is Alex's local copy, read in place and not copied here.
